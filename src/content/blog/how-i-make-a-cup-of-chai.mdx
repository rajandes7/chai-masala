250 ml water, 230 ml 2% milk, 10 g Wagh Bakri tea, 10 g sugar. Aluminum pot, glasstop stove, heat on high throughout. About 9.5 minutes.

1. **Water and tea.** Add 250 ml water and 10 g tea. Heat to a boil (~3 min).
2. **Rolling boil.** Once the surface breaks significantly, boil for ~1.5 min.
3. **Cold milk.** Add 230 ml. Back to a boil (~4 min).
4. **Let it rise.** Once the milk rises, allow ~1 min: two or three rises. Ease the heat each time.
5. **Strain, then sugar.** Stir in 10 g sugar after straining. About 405 ml.

The heating stretches aren't boil time. The boil clock starts when the surface breaks.

## Two cues

- **The rise.** The milk rising is the endpoint. It regulates itself, and it's more reliable than a timer.
- **The color.** Color tracks concentration. Experienced makers judge strength by eye.
